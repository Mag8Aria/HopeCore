# HOPECORE / THE GRASS-HOPER LIES HEAVY

*An archive that can still be answered.*

**Languages:** IT · EN · FR · ES · DE · 中文 · RU · العربية

This is an artistic and documentary project between Internet memory, counter-archives, and possible futures. The repository documents a hypothesis for activation in 2026: starting from fragments of an already public historical archive and imagining a web space where new people can add words, responses, notes, and branches.

This is not about reopening a historical list, reproducing the hopes of a particular season of the Internet, or representing all participants. It is an experiment on what happens when the archive stops being only retrospective.

## 01 / Archives and historical memory

The starting point is a historical collection of messages made available in a public archive. The fragments have been gathered into a single text file to facilitate reading and research. The reunification facilitates comparison between the preserved traces; the originals remain necessary to check their provenance and reconstruction.

Preserving does not automatically mean authorizing a new publication of other people’s words. The complete historical archive remains outside this repository.

## 02 / A moment X, many places

The idea is to open, at a moment X still to be agreed upon, a possibility of simultaneous writing from multiple places, physical or online. A common narrative is not required: responses, disagreements, and new branches may coexist.

The site could host original contributions and responses to authorized historical traces, with pseudonyms, attributions chosen by those who write, and the possibility of requesting corrections or withdrawals. Submitting will not automatically mean publishing: consent, moderation, security, and maintenance capacity will be required.

The site is not yet online; places, date, and participation are not confirmed. For now, adding materials to this repository takes place by invitation or prior agreement with the project direction.
